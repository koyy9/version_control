About the University

A legacy of excellence,
rooted in the fertile
heart of the Philippines.

Central Philippine State University stands as one of the premier state universities in Western Visayas, committed to providing quality, accessible, and relevant higher education. The Main Campus serves as the vibrant hub of academic, cultural, and communal life.

Nestled within a lush, expansive campus, CPSU offers a rare environment where natural beauty and scholarly pursuit coexist in harmony — from its deep agricultural roots to its modern, multi-disciplinary programs spanning technology, health sciences, education, and the arts.

This is your invitation to explore. From canopied walkways and research fields to storied administration buildings and student commons — every corner of CPSU carries a story waiting to be discovered.
